Please, based on the JD provided by the user in this conversation, complete the breakdown using the KSA framework.

Judgment rules:
1. Knowledge refers to "what the position needs to know," such as industry knowledge, business knowledge, regulations, market awareness, etc.
2. Skills refer to "what the position needs to be able to do or which tools to use," such as Excel, SQL, Python, data collection and organization.
3. Abilities refer to "what comprehensive abilities the position needs to demonstrate," such as drilling down business metrics, generating insight recommendations, cross-team collaboration.
4. Basic thresholds like education, years of experience, location, enrollment status, etc., should be placed under basic_requirements and not mixed into KSA.

Granularity control:
1. Only break down requirements that are clearly mentioned or strongly directly expressed in the original JD text; do not freely expand into sub-concepts not explicitly stated in the JD.
2. Do not split a single requirement into multiple synonymous smaller points. For example, "building indicator systems and data dashboards" can be treated as one integrated ability; do not split it into polar star indicators, sub-indicators, caliber documentation, BI tools, etc., unless explicitly mentioned in the JD.
3. Do not add specific metrics or methods not written in the JD, such as DAU, completion rate, p-value, significance testing, recommendation algorithms, etc.; these can appear as explanations in evidence_needed_on_resume but should not be independent requirements.
4. Each KSA item should be of medium granularity, suitable for direct resume matching, neither over-abstracted ability words nor overly fragmented keywords.
5. Keep the output quantity moderate: usually 2-4 knowledge items, 3-6 skills, 2-5 abilities. Only exceed this range if the JD clearly contains more requirements.
