Step 1: Input Personal Experience Database for CariAgent

Please first ensure that your basic information is complete and then try to describe your experiences in detail.

Basic Information to Complete:
1. Name
2. Contact Information
3. School
4. Degree Level
5. Major
6. Graduation Date / Current Enrollment Status
7. Years of Work or Internship Experience
8. Target Employment Direction

These are foundational details; please provide them as fully as possible in one go to avoid repeated follow-ups.

For Experience Entries, you do not need to make them perfect at the start. You can organize them based on the format: “Company / Organization + Role + Time + Specific Tasks.”

Recommended Information to Include:
1. Company or Organization Name
2. Your Role
3. Time Period
4. Projects or Tasks Undertaken
5. Actions You Took
6. Tools Used
7. Results Achieved
8. If available, add data scale, efficiency improvements, business impact, or collaborators involved

Example Format:

Company / Organization:
Role:
Time:
Project / Task:
Actions Taken:
Tools Used:
Results:
Additional Notes:

Regarding “Project Experience,” you can interpret this broadly to include:
- Competitions
- Academic Papers
- Research
- Collaborative Projects
- Personal Products
- Or any experience that reflects a complete logical chain from background, goals, actions to results

Notes:
1. You may also write in natural language rather than strictly following the template.
2. If certain details cannot be recalled at the moment, leave them blank; CariAgent will follow up later.
3. Do not fabricate information for the sake of sounding better; focus on including as much truthful material as possible first.
